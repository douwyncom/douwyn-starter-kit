# Support

## Open-source core

Report reproducible bugs and focused feature proposals by emailing
**[contact@douwyn.com](mailto:contact@douwyn.com)** or through
**[https://douwyn.com](https://douwyn.com)**. Include the starter-kit version,
PHP/database versions, expected behavior, actual behavior, and a minimal
reproduction. Community support is best effort and has no guaranteed response
or resolution time.

Security reports must follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead of a public
issue.

## Commercial modules

For paid modules, licence keys, private registry access, and customer-specific
integration assistance, use **[contact@douwyn.com](mailto:contact@douwyn.com)**,
**[https://douwyn.com](https://douwyn.com)**, or the support channel supplied
with your purchase. Include the module name, licensed version, starter-kit
version, and non-sensitive diagnostic details. Support scope and response times
are governed by the applicable commercial agreement.
