# Code of conduct

We want participation in the Douwyn Starter Kit community to be safe,
respectful, and productive. Treat other participants with empathy, discuss
technical disagreements in good faith, and focus feedback on ideas and work.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, publishing another
person's private information, or sustained disruptive behavior are not
acceptable in project spaces or when representing the project publicly.

Maintainers may edit or remove content and may temporarily or permanently
restrict participation when behavior conflicts with this policy. Enforcement
decisions should consider context, impact, and the safety of participants.

Report conduct concerns privately to
**[contact@douwyn.com](mailto:contact@douwyn.com)**. Reports will be handled as
confidentially as practical. Technical support and bug reports follow
[SUPPORT.md](SUPPORT.md).
